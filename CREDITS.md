## Made for this project

- All code under `Assets/_Project/`.
- Materials and textures under `Assets/_Project/Art/`, generated by `ArtBuilder`.
- Every sound under `Assets/_Project/Audio/`: gunshots, mechanical clicks, impacts, buzzers, the carrier motor and wind. They are synthesised placeholders written out by [`Tools/make_audio.py`](Tools/make_audio.py), so they are original to this project and free to replace with recordings. If recordings come from Freesound, Kenney or the Sonniss GDC bundles, list each one in the table above with its licence.
